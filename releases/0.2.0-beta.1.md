# Spooned OSRS 0.2.0-beta.1

First beta of 0.2 · 13 October 2026

Guides open to everyone, a Support page, rank-coloured names, and a staff portal rebuilt around review. Players can now log out of every device at once.

## For players

- **Guides:** a Guides page with search and tiles. Each guide shows its author, when it was published and when it was last updated. It also has:
  - gear for every budget, side by side;
  - the trip inventory;
  - requirements with their icons, each on one line;
  - live drop tables and odds charts;
  - Wiki links for game terms.
- **Support page:** Supporter and Gilded supporter side by side, each showing how your name looks on the site and in Discord. It also shows where the money goes, the top supporters, and questions that open one at a time.
- **Supporter coin:** every supporter has a silver coin beside their name, and the same coin is the Supporter role icon on Discord. Gilded supporters show the gilded scimitar.
- **Names:** staff names show in their rank colour everywhere. Sir sits right beside the name, and achievement titles start under the username. The owner's name is red, with the inferno scimitar.
- **Banners:** Guides, Leaderboard and Support share one banner. The Leaderboard banner shows the live community totals, and the Guides banner counts published guides.
- **Profiles:**
  - Pets tiles look like collection log tiles;
  - quests show how many are left;
  - character scenes are framed wider;
  - an empty collection timeline shows an example of what it will look like;
  - scroll or pinch on the character to zoom, and turning it eases into place.
- **Guide links:** boss and activity pages show "Read our guide" when a published guide covers them.
- **Log out everywhere:** a button in Account settings ends every signed-in session for your Discord account, on all devices.
- **Credits:** the footer names every data source: the OSRS Hiscores, RuneProfile, TempleOSRS and Wise Old Man.

## For staff

- **Portal tabs:** Overview, Ownership, Corrections, Players, Permissions, Guides and Discord bot.
- **Overview:** review activity for the last 14 days, and a Latest activity feed where each correction and ownership request is one plain sentence.
- **Ownership:** an Awaiting review tab, a step-by-step new request that shows who owns the name now, and every request as a compact row that opens to:
  - an admin notice first, when the account needs one;
  - both accounts;
  - the evidence, with who added it and when;
  - the decision, or a note;
  - a timeline of the request, every note and the decision.

  Staff can hold a name, release a hold, or link a held name to its owner's Discord ID. Accounts with 50+ pets, 500+ log slots, a record, a top-25 board place or a staff owner need an admin.
- **Corrections:** the same layout as Ownership: Awaiting review, step-by-step forms for drop rates, kill rates and XP, and dry-streak records, and a compact history. The drop search lists every boss or activity that drops an item, each with its own rate. Staff can add notes to a correction, and give a reason when approving or rejecting; both join its timeline.
- **Rank rule:** a proposal or request is decided by someone of the proposer's rank or higher, so a moderator can't approve an admin's change.
- **Hold names:** a permission that puts an unprotected name on hold straight away; releasing or linking it still needs a reviewer.
- **Players:** the player count, and names as their profile shows them, with pets, log slots, quest points and donations (amounts a player hides are marked hidden), and actions to:
  - view the profile;
  - open an ownership request;
  - manage staff access;
  - search correction history;
  - fix progress, including the collection log, quests and diaries.
- **Permissions:**
  - access is grouped into Guides, Corrections and Ownership; publishing guides, approving corrections and approving ownership requests are separate permissions;
  - admins manage helpers' and moderators' access;
  - new staff are found by searching a Discord account or RuneScape name, and open as a card with the rank to give;
  - the access history reads one line per change, with filters and a search ("Mira Vale promoted Bramble Pk from Helper to Moderator").
- **Guide editor:**
  - every insert is an icon on an Insert bar, grouped by kind, with its name on hover;
  - type @ in the text to add an item, prayer, spell, skill or quest, and type an s straight after it for the plural;
  - gear tiers are renamed, edited and added in one dialog;
  - Prayers, Spells, and Images or GIFs (uploaded, or by link).
- **Guide workshop:** a search, one-line titles, and new guides that start from a layout (Blank, Bossing, Quest or Diary) suggested from the topic. Quests (with difficulty), diaries and combat achievement tiers can be topics.

## Behind the scenes

- **Migrations:** six new migrations, applied automatically on deploy.
  - `0025_review_permission.sql` adds the approval permission.
  - `0026_publish_ownership_permissions.sql` adds the publishing and ownership permissions.
  - `0027_release_hold_cases.sql` allows release-hold requests.
  - `0028_session_version.sql` adds a session version for Log out everywhere.
  - `0029_hold_permission.sql` adds the Hold names permission.
  - `0030_guide_uploads.sql` records guide uploads and their sizes.
- **Guide image storage:** uploads go to a Cloudflare R2 bucket, capped at 3 MB a file, 25 files (40 MB) per person a day and 1 GB in total. The same picture is stored once.
- **Phones:** staff section tabs scroll in one row, and long row titles drop their details underneath instead of cutting off.
- **Lint:** `npm run lint` runs in CI with no errors allowed.
