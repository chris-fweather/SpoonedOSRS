# Spooned OSRS 0.1.0-beta.3

Third beta · 8 October 2026

The first public source release. This beta gives the site a refreshed icon set, new character scenes and a collection timeline, rebuilds the staff portal and guide editor, and opens the code under the MIT licence.

## For players

- **New icons:** every skill, menu tab, quest and combat tier, chat icon, rank scimitar and account-type badge uses the refreshed icon set. The top three on each board wear gold, silver and bronze crowns, and lava flows through the Owner's scimitar.
- **Account-type badges:** Ironman, Hardcore, Ultimate, Group, Hardcore Group and Unranked Group badges show beside names on profiles, leaderboards and the home page, all at one size. Hovering a badge names the account type.
- **Name blocks:** names read the same everywhere. The scimitar and badge sit level with the name, the achievement title is centred underneath, and a podium crown follows the donation title.
- **Character scenes:** two new scenes, a lantern-lit Mine and a Woodland clearing. Characters now sway gently around the direction you left them facing.
- **Collection timeline:** every dated pet and log slot appears as a lollipop on a zoomable timeline with a month strip. Insights keeps the dry meter and pets by category.
- **Profile settings:**
  - titles and emblems are an even grid of cards, and locked choices explain how to unlock them;
  - visibility now lives in Account settings;
  - saving an outfit never changes your privacy.
- **Account menu:** it shows your Discord picture, and one button syncs your website roles to the SpoonedOSRS Discord server.
- **Smoother loading:**
  - profiles and leaderboards load into their final layout without jumping;
  - Hiscores rank colours are easier to tell apart;
  - skills with no XP never show a rank colour.

## For staff

- **Portal layout:** the staff portal uses the site's horizontal tabs, with one address per tab. Status is shown as amber, green or red dots.
- **Permissions:** a new Permissions tab lets the owner manage the team, rank defaults and per-member access. The server enforces the same rules.
- **Profile staff controls:** set a rank with one tap, switch a profile between public and private, clear titles and badges, and unlink or relink names. Every change is recorded in the account history.
- **Guide editor:**
  - it reads like a document, with gear and inventory setups shown as in-game icons;
  - requirements are picked from items, quests and diaries;
  - text supports colours, lists and the site's emojis;
  - guides open as tiles.

## Behind the scenes

- **Background refresh:** every ten minutes, profiles that never had a full pull get one pass over the Hiscores, RuneProfile, TempleOSRS and Wise Old Man.
- **Leaderboard data:** account types now reach the leaderboards through migration `0024_board_account_mode.sql`, which also backfills existing players.
- **Single address:** the site is served only at spoonedosrs.com; deploys no longer re-enable workers.dev or preview URLs.
- **Open source:** the source is published under the MIT licence. OSRS artwork, Wiki data and fonts keep their own terms; see `docs/third-party-content.md`.
- **Test coverage:** every pull request runs the unit tests, the migration chain, the production build, and the sign-in, staff, guide, privacy and account checks against the built Worker.

Requires migration `0024_board_account_mode.sql`. Linking a name still does not prove RuneScape account ownership.
